A picture behind the launcher's menu, and a widescreen fix in Deep Sea Salvage. Like v0.2.20 it contains no game data: you need your own ROM of the USA release (a big-endian `.z64` of 32 MiB, SHA-1 `166eda1c05670d337e2c3f15a5db528ae1e5d6e3`). Other regions are not supported.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**Windows:** unzip `MarioParty2Recompiled-v0.2.21-windows-x64.zip` and run `MarioParty2Recompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `MarioParty2Recompiled-v0.2.21-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `MarioParty2Recompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2). Pick your ROM with **Load ROM**, then **Start Game**: the ROM is stored, so the next start goes straight to the game.

**New:**

- The launcher's main menu has a picture behind it, made for the project by one of its players, with the title and the menu outlined so they read on it. It covers the window at any shape, cut at its sides in a narrower one ([#106](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-party-2-recomp/issues/106)).

**Fixed:**

- In Deep Sea Salvage, the rocks at both sides of the sea now reach the edges of the 4:3 picture. Before, they stopped just short of them, and thin strips of the sea behind showed down their sides ([#110](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-party-2-recomp/issues/110)).

**Not checked yet:** long play sessions, rumble on a physical pad (it was played in the browser with stand-in pads), and the Linux build on a GPU (it has been booted on Mesa's software Vulkan only). Reports are welcome on the [issues page](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-party-2-recomp/issues).

The [README](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-party-2-recomp#readme) has what works and how to build it yourself. The same game also plays in a browser, with your ROM, at [n64hub.com](https://n64hub.com/#mario-party-2).
