Widescreen fixes for three minigames: Balloon Burst, Filet Relay and Shy Guy Says. Like v0.2.22 it contains no game data: you need your own ROM of the USA release (a big-endian `.z64` of 32 MiB, SHA-1 `166eda1c05670d337e2c3f15a5db528ae1e5d6e3`). Other regions are not supported.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**Windows:** unzip `MarioParty2Recompiled-v0.2.23-windows-x64.zip` and run `MarioParty2Recompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `MarioParty2Recompiled-v0.2.23-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `MarioParty2Recompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2). Pick your ROM with **Load ROM**, then **Start Game**: the ROM is stored, so the next start goes straight to the game.

**Fixed:**

- In Balloon Burst in a wide window, the pressure gauges stay where they belong, with their needles on them. Before, the platforms and gauges behind the players were stretched to the window's width while the needles stayed in the 4:3 middle ([#114](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-party-2-recomp/issues/114)).
- In Filet Relay, a player's view no longer flashes when the fish is handed to the next penguin. On a display faster than the game's 30 frames a second, the frame drawn between two of the game's at each handoff showed the course swept from the old shot to the new one ([#100](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-party-2-recomp/issues/100)).
- In a wide window, Shy Guy Says' near clouds and the rainbow Shy Guy stands on now reach the window's edges. Before, they stopped at 4:3 with a straight cut, and a thin strip of rainbow showed along the bottom ([#109](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-party-2-recomp/issues/109)).

**Not checked yet:** long play sessions, rumble on a physical pad (it was played in the browser with stand-in pads), and the Linux build on a GPU (it has been booted on Mesa's software Vulkan only). Reports are welcome on the [issues page](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-party-2-recomp/issues).

The [README](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-party-2-recomp#readme) has what works and how to build it yourself. The same game also plays in a browser, with your ROM, at [n64hub.com](https://n64hub.com/#mario-party-2).
