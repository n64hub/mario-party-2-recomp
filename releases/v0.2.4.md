The first release of Mario Party 2: Recompiled for Windows and Linux: a native PC port of the N64 game made by static recompilation. It is not an emulator and contains no game data: you need your own ROM of the USA release (a big-endian `.z64` of 32 MiB, SHA-1 `166eda1c05670d337e2c3f15a5db528ae1e5d6e3`). Other regions are not supported.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**What's new since v0.2.3:** fixes for a wide window (Aspect Ratio Expand). In **Lights Out** the lights stay on the players: the minigame draws only the game's own frames, since the darkness is a picture the game redraws each frame and frames blended between them left the lights a step ahead of the players. **Using an item**, the item stays in the character's hand as it moves from the player's panel. In the **1 vs 3** announcement the panels line up. A **board's results** after each minigame keep the board's 4:3 picture with black at its sides and no panels over the black. **Bowl Over** and **Bobsled Run** play at 4:3, as their two views are drawn for it.

**Windows:** unzip `MarioParty2Recompiled-v0.2.4-windows-x64.zip` and run `MarioParty2Recompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `MarioParty2Recompiled-v0.2.4-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `MarioParty2Recompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2). Pick your ROM with **Load ROM**, then **Start Game**: the ROM is stored, so the next start goes straight to the game.

**What works:** the menus, its six boards and the minigames, with sound, the game's EEPROM saves kept across restarts, and keyboard and gamepad input. Up to four people play, each with a pad of their own or the keyboard (each pad its own player with no setup, or the menu's Controls, Assign players).

**Enhancements**, each an option on the settings menu with an Original value that plays as the console does:

- Faster board movement and map scrolling (General tab)
- High resolution at the window's size
- Smooth motion at the display's refresh rate, the boards' cameras and pieces blended between the game's frames
- Widescreen: each board's view and background reach the window's edges and the players' panels its corners; scenes the game draws for 4:3 only keep black at the sides, and a minigame whose stage ends short of a wide window's edges is drawn out to where it ends
- Keyboard and gamepad controls, rebindable

**Not checked yet:** long play sessions, rumble on a pad, and the Linux build on a GPU (it has been booted on Mesa's software Vulkan only). Reports are welcome on the [issues page](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-party-2-recomp/issues).

The [README](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-party-2-recomp#readme) has what works and how to build it yourself. The same game also plays in a browser, with your ROM, at [n64hub.com](https://n64hub.com/#mario-party-2).
