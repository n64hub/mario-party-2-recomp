Three widescreen fixes, one of them a crash, and a flash on the stairway up to Mini-Game Land. Like v0.2.19 it contains no game data: you need your own ROM of the USA release (a big-endian `.z64` of 32 MiB, SHA-1 `166eda1c05670d337e2c3f15a5db528ae1e5d6e3`). Other regions are not supported.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**Windows:** unzip `MarioParty2Recompiled-v0.2.20-windows-x64.zip` and run `MarioParty2Recompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `MarioParty2Recompiled-v0.2.20-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `MarioParty2Recompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2). Pick your ROM with **Load ROM**, then **Start Game**: the ROM is stored, so the next start goes straight to the game.

**Fixed:**

- In a wide window, the cards that slide in while a game is set up (the clouds for the number of players, the character banners, the skill levels, the Bonus choice) now slide all the way to the screen's edges. Before, they were cut off sharply at the 4:3 edges while the rails they hang from went on ([#97](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-party-2-recomp/issues/97)).
- In a wide window, the opening's theater now fills the screen: its red curtains and pink borders reach the window's edges (up to 16:9), and the painted backdrop is widened to meet the curtains. Before, the stage stopped short of the edges, and the characters waiting to come on, or just gone off, showed in the black beside it ([#92](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-party-2-recomp/issues/92)).
- The game no longer overwrites its own memory when a board's game begins in a wide window, which crashed the browser page at Western Land's start. Each sprite drawn out to the window's edges takes a few more drawing commands, and at Western Land's start a frame ran past the room the game sets aside for them, overwriting its memory; each frame's commands now have room to spare.
- Climbing the stairway up to Mini-Game Land, the brick arch overhead no longer flashes for a moment sharp and unfogged, nearer than it is, as the climb starts ([#95](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-party-2-recomp/issues/95)).

**Not checked yet:** long play sessions, rumble on a physical pad (it was played in the browser with stand-in pads), and the Linux build on a GPU (it has been booted on Mesa's software Vulkan only). Reports are welcome on the [issues page](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-party-2-recomp/issues).

The [README](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-party-2-recomp#readme) has what works and how to build it yourself. The same game also plays in a browser, with your ROM, at [n64hub.com](https://n64hub.com/#mario-party-2).
