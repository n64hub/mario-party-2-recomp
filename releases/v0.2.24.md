Widescreen and high-resolution fixes: screen wipes, the block-stack item minigame, Speed Hockey, Sky Pilots and Crazy Cutters. Like v0.2.23 it contains no game data: you need your own ROM of the USA release (a big-endian `.z64` of 32 MiB, SHA-1 `166eda1c05670d337e2c3f15a5db528ae1e5d6e3`). Other regions are not supported.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**Windows:** unzip `MarioParty2Recompiled-v0.2.24-windows-x64.zip` and run `MarioParty2Recompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `MarioParty2Recompiled-v0.2.24-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `MarioParty2Recompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2). Pick your ROM with **Load ROM**, then **Start Game**: the ROM is stored, so the next start goes straight to the game.

**Fixed:**

- In a wide window, screen wipes (the star, Toad, Mushroom, Bowser and ? wipes) now sweep the whole window. Before, a closing wipe left the board showing past 4:3 until the screen cut to black, and an opening one left black bars at the sides until it ended ([#102](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-party-2-recomp/issues/102)).
- In a wide window, after an item is won in the item minigame where block stacks in a ring are knocked down with a mallet, Baby Bowser no longer stands in the black beside the picture after he leaps off its top left corner; the minigame is now drawn at 4:3, with black at its sides ([#111](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-party-2-recomp/issues/111)).
- Speed Hockey is drawn at 4:3 in a wide window, with black at its sides, like the other minigames played on a picture of the screen's size. Before, the rink was stretched to the window's edges while the lit score lights on its top wall stayed at their 4:3 places, so they sat over the edges of the inner sockets and left the outer ones empty ([#116](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-party-2-recomp/issues/116)).
- In a wide window, the strip of clouds between Sky Pilots' two views now reaches the window's edges, with the teams' icons and the finish post placed along it. Before, it stopped at 4:3 with plain white bars on both sides ([#115](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-party-2-recomp/issues/115)).
- In Crazy Cutters at a resolution above the console's, no thin dark line shows under each player's drill any more. The drill and the chips flying off it are drawn on a square that reached a quarter of a texel past the bottom of its picture, into whatever lay after it, which is too thin to see at 320x240 but a line of a pixel or two in a large window ([#118](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-party-2-recomp/issues/118)).

**Not checked yet:** long play sessions, rumble on a physical pad (it was played in the browser with stand-in pads), and the Linux build on a GPU (it has been booted on Mesa's software Vulkan only). Reports are welcome on the [issues page](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-party-2-recomp/issues).

The [README](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-party-2-recomp#readme) has what works and how to build it yourself. The same game also plays in a browser, with your ROM, at [n64hub.com](https://n64hub.com/#mario-party-2).
